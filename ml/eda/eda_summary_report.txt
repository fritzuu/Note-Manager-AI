║  2. KEY FINDINGS                                                             ║
║  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────  ║
║  • Study hours show the STRONGEST positive correlation with exam score       ║
║    (r = +0.825).  Each additional study hour per day is associated     ║
║    with a measurable increase in exam performance.                           ║
║                                                                              ║
║  • Attendance percentage is a close second (r = +0.090).               ║
║    Students attending >85% of classes score notably higher on average.      ║
║                                                                              ║
║  • Social media consumption exhibits a NEGATIVE relationship (r = -0.167).  ║
║    Students averaging >3 h/day of social media tend to score lower.         ║
║                                                                              ║
║  • Mental health rating shows a POSITIVE correlation (r = +0.322),          ║
║    underscoring the role of psychological well-being in academic success.   ║
║                                                                              ║
║  • Approximately 27.7% of students are classified as Excellent (≥80)   ║
║    while 13.1% fall into the Low (<50) category.                          ║
